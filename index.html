Twakane board

Private tool for the Hon. Stephen Nzyoki Sila (Twakane) campaign team, Kangundo West.

The Twakane board is a private web app used by a small campaign team. Team members send photos and updates from the field. A verifier checks them, and approved posts are published to the campaign's Facebook Page. The board also collects Machakos County news for the team to review.

It is not open to the public. Only people added by the campaign's social media lead can sign in.
