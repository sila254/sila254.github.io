Privacy policy

Twakane board · last updated 23 November 2026

The Twakane board is a private tool used by the campaign team of Hon. Stephen Nzyoki Sila (Twakane) to collect, check and publish campaign updates. It is run by the campaign's social media lead, Mark Sila (silamark.ms@gmail.com).

What the board keeps

  • Team members: name, phone number, role on the board, and a scrambled (hashed) copy of their sign-in PIN. The PIN itself is never stored.
  • Posts: photos, videos, links and captions team members send, who sent them, when, and whether they were approved, rejected or published.
  • An activity log of who did what on the board, so the lead can check actions.
  • County news: headlines and links gathered from public news sites.

What it is used for

Only to run the campaign's updates: reviewing submissions, publishing approved ones to the campaign's Facebook Page, and keeping a record. The data is not sold, rented or shared for advertising.

Where it is stored

  • Records are kept in Google Cloud Firestore in a project owned by the campaign.
  • Photos, videos and the activity log are kept in the campaign's Google Drive.
  • Approved posts are published publicly on the campaign's Facebook Page.
  • The board itself runs on Render (render.com).

Google account access

The board signs in to the campaign's own Google account to store its data. It asks Google for two permissions only: access to the Drive files it creates itself (it cannot see any other files in that Drive), and access to the campaign's Firestore database. It does not read email, contacts or other Google data.

Who can see it

Only team members the lead has added, and only what their role allows. Contributors see their own submissions. Verifiers and the lead see what they need to review and publish.

Keeping and deleting

Data is kept for the length of the campaign and then deleted. Any team member can ask to see the information held about them, have it corrected, or be removed from the board, by contacting silamark.ms@gmail.com. These rights follow Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.
